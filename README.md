## 🎯 What Burning Studio Does

Burning Studio is built around a simple promise: give you professional results without a professional learning curve. The application groups its tools into clear categories so you always know where to look.

**Disc creation.** Build data discs, audio CDs, video DVDs, and mixed-mode discs. The app checks your files before writing so you do not waste a blank disc on a mistake.

**Copying.** Duplicate an existing disc, or copy a disc directly to an image file on your computer so you can burn it again later without hunting down the original.

**Imaging.** Create ISO and other image formats from folders, and write existing image files back to physical media.

**Backup and archiving.** Save important folders into a compressed archive that can span multiple discs if the data is large. The app tells you in advance how many discs you will need.

**Erasing and formatting.** Wipe rewritable discs clean so they can be reused, and prepare new media for writing.

**Verification.** After writing, the app can read the disc back and compare it against the source data, so you know the copy is sound before you file it away.

## 🛠️ Tips for Best Results

- **Use quality media.** Cheap discs fail more often, especially at high speeds.
- **Burn at a moderate speed** (8x–16x for DVDs). Fast burns are quicker but less reliable on older hardware.
- **Keep verification switched on** for anything important. It adds a few minutes and saves a lot of regret.
- **Store projects, not just discs.** Saving your project means you can rebuild the same disc in seconds.
- **Close other heavy applications** while burning to keep the data stream steady.
- **Keep image files on a drive with plenty of free space.** A dual-layer image can be several gigabytes.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

**The app will not open after installation.** Restart your computer, then try again from the Start menu. Make sure you completed the setup to the final screen.

**The drive is not detected.** Check that the drive has power and a disc inserted, then use the Refresh button in the drive dropdown. Unplug and reconnect external drives.

**"Insert a blank disc" keeps appearing.** Confirm the disc is blank and matches the project type — a DVD project will not accept a CD.

**The burn fails partway through.** Try a lower write speed, a different brand of disc, and close other programs. Reboot and retry.

**Verification reports errors.** The disc may be faulty or dirty. Try a fresh disc before assuming the drive is at fault.
